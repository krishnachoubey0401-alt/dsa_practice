// AI in Education (Impact of AI on Student Learning) - Discussion Notes & Speaking Script

/* 
 * 1. INTRODUCTION (Shuruaat kaise karni hai)
 * ----------------------------------------------------
 * Tu mere jagah hota to ye bolta:
 */
"Hello everyone. Today's topic is Artificial Intelligence in Education. AI is 
rapidly changing how students learn and how teachers educate. In my view, AI is 
a powerful tool that can greatly enhance learning if used responsibly."


/* 
 * 2. ADVANTAGES / PROS (Fayde kya bolne hain)
 * ----------------------------------------------------
 * Tu mere jagah hota to ye points rakhta:
 */
// Point A: Personalized Learning
"First, AI provides personalized learning. It adapts to each student's pace, 
helping them understand complex concepts through customized explanations and examples."

// Point B: 24/7 Availability
"Second, AI tools act like a personal tutor available 24/7. Students can ask 
doubts anytime, get instant feedback, and practice problem-solving at their convenience."

// Point C: Efficiency for Teachers
"Third, it helps teachers automate repetitive tasks like grading and administrative 
work, giving them more time to focus on teaching and guiding students."


/* 
 * 3. DISADVANTAGES / CHALLENGES (Nuksan ya Dikkat kya hoti hai)
 * ----------------------------------------------------
 * Tu mere jagah hota to ye flaws point out karta:
 */
// Point A: Over-dependence & Reduced Critical Thinking
"However, the biggest risk is over-dependence. If students rely on AI to write 
their code, essays, or solve every problem, their critical thinking and creativity will suffer."

// Point B: Lack of Emotional Intelligence
"Also, AI cannot replace the human element in teaching. Empathy, mentorship, 
and real-life guidance from a human teacher are crucial for holistic development."

// Point C: Quality & Accuracy Issues
"Lastly, AI can sometimes generate incorrect or biased information, so students 
must verify facts rather than blindly trusting the output."


/* 
 * 4. CONCLUSION (End kaise karna hai)
 * ----------------------------------------------------
 * Tu mere jagah hota to final stance ye leta:
 */
"To conclude, AI should be viewed as an assistant, not a replacement for thinking. 
When used to clear doubts and speed up learning rather than to skip effort, AI can be 
an incredible asset for education."
